Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

FVeedback is a personal, non-commercial application used exclusively by its owner to control their own devices through their own self-hosted Home Assistant instance.

Data accessed

Through the Google Smart Device Management API, FVeedback reads and changes the status of the owner's own Nest thermostat (temperature, humidity, mode and setpoint).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to control the owner's heating and cooling.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's local server. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking is used.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

Informativa sulla privacy

FVeedback è un'applicazione personale e non commerciale, usata esclusivamente dal proprietario per comandare i propri dispositivi tramite la propria istanza Home Assistant, ospitata in casa. Tramite la Google Smart Device Management API legge e modifica lo stato del proprio termostato Nest (temperatura, umidità, modalità, setpoint). I dati restano solo sul server locale del proprietario, non vengono venduti né condivisi con terzi, e non sono usati per pubblicità o analisi. L'accesso si può revocare in qualsiasi momento dalle impostazioni dell'account Google.
